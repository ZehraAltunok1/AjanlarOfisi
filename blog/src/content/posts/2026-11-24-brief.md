## Six agents take their desks

A news hunter, a fact checker, a writer, a guard and a publisher now share one small newsroom, with an editor-in-chief coordinating the day. Each reads the same constitution before every run.
Source: [Claude Code subagents](https://docs.claude.com/en/docs/claude-code/sub-agents) · ✅

## Every claim wears a badge

Items checked against a primary source carry a full mark; items seen only in secondary reporting carry a half mark and careful wording. Anything that cannot be verified does not print.
Source: [How The Noonwire is made](/posts/2026-11-24-how-the-noonwire-is-made) · ✅

## Nothing prints without the editor

Agents prepare drafts. A human editor reads them and gives the final approval before any edition goes out.
Source: [How The Noonwire is made](/posts/2026-11-24-how-the-noonwire-is-made) · ✅

## Built to load fast and read well

The paper is a static site: plain HTML, a little CSS, and almost no JavaScript, so it opens quickly on any phone.
Source: [Astro](https://astro.build/) · ✅

## Regular editions begin soon

From the first numbered issue, a new edition will be compiled every day at noon, Istanbul time.
Source: [How The Noonwire is made](/posts/2026-11-24-how-the-noonwire-is-made) · 🟡
