## 6. Cisco and OpenAI open-source Shirudo, an eBPF "shield" for unpatched exploits
- What: Shirudo is the first project of Cisco and OpenAI's Secure Infrastructure Project. It is rooted in Isovalent's Tetragon and deploys small, signed eBPF mitigations to running Linux systems while full patches roll out. OpenAI says it uses Shirudo in its Codex-powered agentic control plane.
